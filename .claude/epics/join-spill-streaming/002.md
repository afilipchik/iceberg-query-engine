# Task: Stream the join output (phase A per batch, phase B per chunk)

## Description

`execute_spill_path` currently returns `stream::iter(all_results)` after
materializing every matched batch. Restructure into a stream with
per-call state: phase A consumes probe batches, probes resident
partitions and yields matched batches immediately (spilling probe rows
for spilled partitions as now); phase A' (build-side SEMI/ANTI) yields
the resident partitions' final emission once the probe stream ends;
phase B iterates spilled partitions and yields per chunk (INNER,
build-side SEMI/ANTI) or per partition (probe-side SEMI/ANTI bitmap).
Probe spill writers are closed exactly once between A and B; probe files
are per call and removed with the operator (Drop) as today. Trace lines
(START/probe collected/DONE) keep their meaning.
