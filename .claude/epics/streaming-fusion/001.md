Key insight vs the PRD's premise: the aggregate's expression evaluation
does NOT vanish under fusion — it relocates onto the same 32-core
budget (the fused streaming agg already overlaps). What fusion can
actually remove: the intermediate match/index vectors (probe emits
matches Vec<(u32,u32,u32)>, then converts to Vec<(usize,usize)> +
Vec<usize> — ~24GB of write+readback traffic on Q9's two 604M-row
joins), per-batch RecordBatch assembly, and the channel hop.
**Ceiling ≈ 2.5-3.5s suite-wide, ~2s of it in Q9. GO**, with 002
re-scoped into two levers:

- **Lever A (low risk)**: kill the intermediate vectors — probe emits
  directly into u32 take-index arrays consumed by create_joined_batch
  (single-batch builds: batch idx is always 0).
- **Lever B (optional, higher risk)**: scalar fused sink for the
  Q9-shape (tiny group count, SUM over numerics): accumulate per match
  from probe arrays + row store, no arrays materialized. Only if A
  leaves the ceiling unclaimed.
