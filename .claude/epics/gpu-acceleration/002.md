# Task: Pricing bench and GO/NO-GO

## GO/NO-GO: **GO** (measured 2026-08-22, RTX 5090, driver 580)

| shape (60M rows, SF=10 lineitem scale) | GPU warm | CPU (rayon, 32 threads) | speedup |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q6: 3-pred filter + SUM | 0.266ms | 8.90ms | **33.5x** |
| Q1-flat: arith chain, 4 SUMs | 1.234ms | 34.8ms | **28.2x** |

H2D (pageable): 11.8 GB/s -> cold Q6 including upload share ~143ms — cold
LOSES, exactly the PCIe theory. Consequence baked into the design: GPU runs
ONLY over resident columns; first touch stays on the CPU path and kicks off
a background upload. Checksums matched bit-exact on these shapes.
libnvrtc comes from the repo .venv's nvidia pip wheel (CUDA 12.8, Blackwell-
capable); LD_LIBRARY_PATH must include it for gpu builds.
