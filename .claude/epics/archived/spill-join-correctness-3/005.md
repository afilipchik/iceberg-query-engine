### Observations for the backlog (none are gate failures)

- The join spill path is correct but slow at SF=100: Q09 ~1,400s at
  both 1G and 256M; Q18 355s at 256M; Q03 188s at 256M. Two known causes,
  both pre-existing and documented: the probe side is fully materialized
  before probing, and spilled partitions are processed one at a time on
  one thread. Natural next join-spill task.
- 256M is where the two remaining spill-path boundaries become visible
  on TPC-H (LEFT join spill in Q20; ON-clause filter spill in Q21). Both
  refuse cleanly by name.
- Native at 1G surfaces the THIRD documented boundary (oom-safety-
  hardening 004's): a native table whose on-disk size exceeds the budget
  is only streamed segment-at-a-time into aggregate-covered consumers;
  when it feeds a join (Q02 part, Q10 customer, Q11/Q20 partsupp, Q15
  lineitem) it refuses by name. Making `NativeTable::scan()` spill-aware
  for join consumers is the corresponding backlog item. On parquet the
  same queries complete at 1G (the parquet scan streams), so this is a
  provider gap, not an operator gap.
