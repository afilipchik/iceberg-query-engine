## Outcome addendum (follow-up, 2026-09-05)

build / 20M probe / 256MB budget) completed only on the rlimit lever at the
default 1G cap and were killed on the cgroup lever (LEFT build_right=0 at

same fixture. Root-caused with per-second RSS timelines aligned to the
`QE_SPILL_DEBUG` phase traces and a one-variable A/B (see
`updates/003/stream-A.md`): the excess appears at the start of phase B and is
`gather_column`'s one-array-per-output-row gather (a 1-row `compute::take` per

gather. Fix: one `compute::interleave` (or one `take`) per column, and every
gathered emission sliced to `SPILL_EMIT_ROWS` = 8,192 rows so the 8-batch
output channel bounds bytes, not just batch count (`QE_SPILL_EMIT_ROWS` /
`QE_SPILL_GATHER=legacy` are the diagnostic switches that reproduce the A/B on
the shipped binary). Peaks (8G scope, same sampler): left-join br0 1,180 →
